BORGAS designs and builds field diagnostic instruments and racing electronics.

Diagnostic Systems

Instruments for field-service troubleshooting of industrial equipment: independent external measurement, guided tests, event capture and fault isolation that complement a machine's own diagnostics rather than repeating them.

BFD-01

Field Diagnostic Instrument

Handheld voltage, resistance, continuity, digital and frequency measurement with a guided diagnostic library for specific equipment, live capture, and an event log. Updates and configuration through the BORGAS Device Manager.

BPS-01

Power System

Rechargeable battery and power subsystem for BORGAS diagnostic equipment in the field, with live battery telemetry on its own display.

Racing Systems

Dashboard and signalling hardware for sim racing, driven live from the simulator's telemetry.

BRC-02

Racing Dashboard

Colour dashboard with selectable layouts, RPM and shift lighting, session flags and lap timing, fed over USB from SimHub.

BRC-FL

Flag Panel

Companion LED flag panel showing session flags from the same telemetry feed.

Software

Every BORGAS instrument is managed by one application.

BORGAS DEVICE MANAGER

Windows application

Detects connected BORGAS devices, reads their identity, and installs verified firmware updates - with checksum verification before anything is written and confirmation of what is running afterwards. Published firmware is distributed from github.com/borgas123/borgas-firmware.
