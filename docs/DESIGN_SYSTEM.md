# Design system
The brief's website palette is used: page #080C14, surface #101927, raised #152235, text #F4F7FB/#B1C1D6, action #7C3AED, cyan #47DFF2, violet #B9A0FF. Semantic tokens live in src/styles/tokens.css.
Inter 400/500/600 and Space Grotesk 500/600 are locally bundled Latin fonts under their supplied OFL licenses. Copies of licenses are in public/licenses.
Use the existing spacing scale, 1200 px maximum content width, 48 px actions and responsive grids. Reduced motion disables transitions and smooth scrolling.
The supplied raven is black artwork: retain its entire lockup, proportions and lettering on a white tile. Do not mask, recolor, crop or substitute a typeset wordmark. Header is taller than the brief default to keep the complete emblem readable.
No genuine product photography is available. Home uses the approved brand mark. Firmware display renders are labeled. Text-only product artwork is typographic, not an invented device.
Gallery images are normal links: they remain usable without JavaScript. Navigation is a disclosure, with Escape and focus restoration. Without JavaScript, mobile links stay visible.
